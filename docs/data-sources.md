Relevant text: the Site is copyright; material may be downloaded, displayed, printed and reproduced in unaltered form only for personal, non-commercial use or use within an organisation; "Information on the Site may not be reproduced in whole or in part for general publication or commercial purposes without the permission of the Host"; any reproduction or use of content without express written consent, unless authorised by law, is prohibited.
Conclusion: publishing PassMark scores on a public site needs the Host's permission. Not collected. STOP per plan; user decision requested.

## Blender Open Data (opendata.blender.org)
About page: https://opendata.blender.org/about/ - states the data is "License-free" (not subject to copyright), downloadable as JSON/CSV, anonymous, no registration. Score = Cycles path-tracing samples per minute (rendering), so it measures rendering throughput, not gaming FPS.
